Yahtzee Roller

Yahtzee is played with five six-sided dice. Roll all five, set aside any you want to keep, and roll again to chase a scoring combination — three of a kind, a straight, a full house, and so on. This roller handles the dice for you: press Roll dice (or hit Enter) to throw all five at once. The tray and the readout below always show your latest roll.
